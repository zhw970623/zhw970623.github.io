Generative Trajectories and Multi-UAV Formation Navigation

Haowen Zhang · Research and system implementation. Connecting trajectory learning, policy optimization and distributed real-world execution.

Learning pipeline ↓Real-world validation ↓

Research overview

Multi-UAV navigation must support individual goals while maintaining coordination and collision avoidance. This project explores conditional flow matching (CFM), DAgger data feedback and MAPPO policy optimization in a pipeline from generative trajectory learning to distributed execution.

The engineering work focuses on consistency between sampling and policy updates, feasibility constraints and safe fallback behavior in real flight. Closed-loop validation has been completed with three Sunray300 UAVs.

Learning and execution pipeline

  1. 01 · Collect dataSimulation, expert demonstrations and DAgger feedback
  2. 02 · Generate & optimizeConditional flow matching and policy learning
  3. 03 · Validate & executeDistributed flight with feasibility checks
  • Data feedback. Iteratively collect data from states visited by the current policy to reduce the difference between demonstration and execution-state distributions.
  • Policy consistency. Align rollout sampling and policy updates to preserve the on-policy assumptions of PPO-style methods.
  • Execution constraints. Integrate learned policies, explicit feasibility checks and fallback behavior into the real-world navigation loop.

Validation scope

The current evidence is an end-to-end system implementation and distributed real-world closed-loop validation with three Sunray300 UAVs, focusing on the connection between trajectory generation, policy optimization and coordinated execution.
