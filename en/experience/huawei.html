What I worked on

My work focused on the Bisheng / LLVM compiler toolchain and performance optimization for C/C++ applications on AArch64. I investigated compilation, linking and runtime bottlenecks, including LTO / ThinLTO, and connected optimization work with validation in the product environment.

The emphasis was not only on faster execution, but also on build efficiency and binary size. This experience provided a foundation for my later work on efficient policy deployment.
